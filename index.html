    FAQ

    Which business types do you work with?

    Sole proprietors and LLCs. Sole proprietors on the single tax; LLCs on the single tax or the general system, with or without VAT.

    Can I come to you for just one service?

    Yes, many things can be handled separately: opening or closing a sole proprietorship, cash registers, a single report or bank financial monitoring. We’ll figure out what fits your business at the consultation.

    Do you work with LLCs on the general system with VAT?

    Yes. We keep books for LLCs on the general system, with or without VAT, plus export and import operations and registering and unblocking tax invoices.

    Do you support sole proprietors with employees?

    Yes. We handle hiring paperwork and employment contracts, payroll, timesheets, sick and maternity leave, dismissals and reports.

    Can you help set up management accounting?

    Yes. We build management reporting for the owner: P&L, Cash Flow, Balance, plus control forms tailored to your business.

    We already have bookkeeping but no financial system. Can you help?

    Yes. We start by analysing how everything works now, find the bottlenecks and suggest what to change in accounting, document flow and control.

    How does working together start?

    With a consultation. Then we get to know the business, analyse the real situation, build a solution and support it.

    When are you available?

    On working days, until 5 pm. At weekends the team rests: we believe people who rest properly work well. As the owner, Olena makes sure the work is organised so everything gets done well and on time within working hours.
